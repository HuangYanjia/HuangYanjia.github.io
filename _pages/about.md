I am **Yanjia Huang**, **Founder and CEO of <a href="https://envora.world/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Envora</a>**, a company focused on **Physical AI**. I am also a **Ph.D. student in Computer Science at UCLA**, currently **on leave**, and a member of the <a href="https://www.math.ucla.edu/aivc/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">AIVC Lab</a>, advised by <a href="https://www.math.ucla.edu/~cffjiang/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Chenfanfu Jiang</a>.

Previously, I pursued a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering at **Texas A&M University** as a member of the <a href="https://taco-group.github.io/" target="_blank">TACO Group</a>, advised by Prof. <a href="https://vztu.github.io/" target="_blank">Zhengzhong Tu</a>. I left the doctoral program with a **master's degree in Mechanical Engineering**.

My research explores how ***world model***, ***policy learning from videos*** can be woven together to produce robust, long-horizon plans for **Embodied Agents**, from indoor navigation to dexterous manipulation.

I earned dual B.Eng. degrees in Mechanical Engineering from <a href="http://www.sbcen.usst.edu.cn/" target="_blank">SBC, USST</a> and <a href="https://www.ljmu.ac.uk/" target="_blank">LJMU</a>, with a minor in Physics. My previous research experience includes <a href="https://www.fudan.edu.cn/en/" target="_blank">Fudan University</a> (Prof. <a href="http://yanweifu.github.io/" target="_blank">Yanwei Fu</a>), NYU's <a href="https://ai4ce.github.io/" target="_blank">AI4CE</a> and <a href="https://yifang.org/group.html" target="_blank">MMVC</a> labs (Profs. <a href="https://engineering.nyu.edu/faculty/chen-feng" target="_blank">Chen Feng</a> and <a href="https://nyuad.nyu.edu/en/academics/divisions/engineering/faculty/yi-fang.html" target="_blank">Yi Fang</a>), and an internship at <a href="https://www.huawei.com/en/corporate-information/research-development" target="_blank">Huawei's Noah's Ark Lab</a> with <a href="https://xuhangcn.github.io/" target="_blank">Hang Xu</a>.

Outside of research, I'm fond of <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wuY20YN6F4k" target="_blank">Bach</a>, play piano and guitar, read Oscar Wilde's poetry and the Stoics, and unwind with basketball, cycling, and the occasional daydream.
